A master course (6 EC) at LIACS on large language models that reason, act and interact. We follow the textbook [*Agentic Large Language Models*](/publication/2026-10-09-Agentic-Large-Language-Models) (Van Duijn, Van der Meer, Plaat & Van Stein) and teach the course together as the same four authors.

The course website, with the syllabus, the weekly schedule and announcements, is at [agenticllms.liacs.nl](https://agenticllms.liacs.nl/){:target="_blank"}.

## What the course covers

The course follows the parts of the book:

- **Language modelling:** the foundations of LLMs, the training pipeline, scaling laws and efficient models.
- **Reasoning:** reasoning models; vision, action and world models; metalearning.
- **Agents:** interaction styles for agentic LLMs.
- **Tools and applications:** search and retrieval, how agents use tools to act in the world, and real-world assistants.
- **Behavioural and cognitive perspectives:** studying the behaviour of agentic LLMs, role-based interaction in multi-LLM systems, and emergent behaviour in multi-agent environments.
- **Advanced topics:** synthetic data generation, mechanistic interpretability for LLM agents, and cognitive perspectives.

## How it works

There is no written exam. Students work on four hands-on Python case studies: building an LLM from scratch, reasoning and self-reflection with the BALROG benchmark, a multi-agent diagnosis framework, and simulating societies of LLM agents. The final grade is the average of the four.

Students are expected to have a background in Python and PyTorch, classical machine learning, deep learning, reinforcement learning and NLP basics; the first part of the course reviews the fundamentals.

## A course that moves with the field

Agentic LLMs change from one semester to the next. We plan to update the book every year, and to update the course with it.
