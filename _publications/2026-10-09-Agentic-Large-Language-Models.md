abstract: >-
  Large language models (LLMs) have taken the world by storm. Their ability to communicate with us in
  our own language, and to reason about our world, has made them a favorite tool for writing,
  answering questions, and much more. Their answers already influence our decisions and our actions.
  What would happen if LLMs themselves could act in the world directly? What would happen if LLMs
  could interact with each other? Agentic LLMs combine the power of large language models with the
  ability to act in the real world. In this book we will discuss how agentic LLMs work, how they act,
  how they interact, and what this means for us, as users.
