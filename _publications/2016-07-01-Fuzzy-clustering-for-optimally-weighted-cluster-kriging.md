abstract: >-
  Kriging or Gaussian Process Regression has been successfully applied in many fields. One of the
  major bottlenecks of Kriging is the complexity in both processing time (cubic) and memory
  (quadratic) in the number of data points. To overcome these limitations, a variety of approximation
  algorithms have been proposed. One of these approximation algorithms is Optimally Weighted Cluster
  Kriging (OWCK). In this paper, OWCK is extended and enhanced by the use of fuzzy clustering methods
  in order to increase the accuracy. Several options are proposed and evaluated against both the
  original OWCK and a variety of other Kriging approximation algorithms.
