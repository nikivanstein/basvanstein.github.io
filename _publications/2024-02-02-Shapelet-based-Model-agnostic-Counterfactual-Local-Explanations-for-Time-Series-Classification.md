abstract: >-
  In this work, we propose a model-agnostic instance-based post-hoc explainability method for time
  series classification. The proposed algorithm, namely Time-CF, leverages shapelets and TimeGAN to
  provide counterfactual explanations for arbitrary time series classifiers. We validate the proposed
  method on several real-world univariate time series classification tasks from the UCR Time Series
  Archive. The results indicate that the counterfactual instances generated by Time-CF when compared
  to state-of-the-art methods, demonstrate better performance in terms of four explainability metrics:
  closeness, sensibility, plausibility, and sparsity.
