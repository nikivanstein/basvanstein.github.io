abstract: >-
  Anomaly detection describes methods of finding abnormal states, instances or data points that differ
  from a normal value space. Industrial processes are a domain where predicitve models are needed for
  finding anomalous data instances for quality enhancement. A main challenge, however, is absence of
  labels in this environment. This paper contributes to a data-centric way of approaching artificial
  intelligence in industrial production. With a use case from additive manufacturing for automotive
  components we present a deep-learning-based image processing pipeline. We integrate the concept of
  domain randomisation and synthetic data in the loop that shows promising results for bridging
  advances in deep learning and its application to real-world, industrial production processes.
