**Large AI models for a resilient high-tech industry.** Foundation for Industry (FIND) brings together 5 universities with 10 labs, 11 Dutch companies, ranging from start-ups to multinationals, and 2 knowledge institutes to pave the way for a new wave of AI-based automation that helps the Dutch industry strengthen and keep its international competitive advantage as a leading high-tech nation in the AI-era.

FIND brings the benefits of foundation models, i.e. large AI models, to industry-relevant applications and data types that are not yet well served, in domains such as health tech, smart industry, and mobility and mobile robotics. These applications need foundation models beyond the current state of the art: more robust and transparent, able to work with new data modalities such as industrial sensor and time-series data, and able to run on edge devices for faster decisions and better privacy.

The programme will release an open-source toolbox of pre-trained models, code for training and integration, and validated research outputs, so that companies outside the consortium can use the technology too. Industry partners are involved through the User Committee, and an Ethical & Societal Impact board oversees the wider effects of the work.

FIND runs from 2025 to 2031 and is funded through the [NWO Perspectief](https://www.nwo.nl/en/projects/hxiii82016) programme. More about the programme, its partners and its people is on the [FIND website](https://foundation4industry.nl/) and the [Leiden University project page](https://www.universiteitleiden.nl/en/research/research-projects/science/liacs-foundation-for-industry-find).
